03. Clinical Excellence

The CRTL Support Framework

Developed in-house by Vital Healthcare’s founders, the Community Reintegration/Rehabilitation & Transition Living (CRTL) framework delivers predictable, compassionate support.

Phase 01

Multidisciplinary Formulation

In-depth assessment of clinical history, communication profiles, sensory triggers, and personal aspirations prior to placement.

Phase 02

Graduated Transition

The Rehabilition step ensures that transitioning from institutions or other placements or an environment without structure is as seamless as possible.

Phase 03

Active Skill Acquisition

Daily routines structured around cooking, money management, self-care, social activities, and positive behavioural reinforcement which help with the transition phase.

Phase 04

Sustainable Independence

Step-down of support hours where appropriate, community employment, college enrollment, and lifelong self-advocacy.

04. Qualified Professionals

About VHC

Vital HealthCare is a Supported Living & Domiciliary Care company with community rehabilitation and supported living resources Nationwide. We specialise in providing holistic and comprehensive care packages for people with learning disabilities, Asperger's/autistic spectrum disorders, acute Brain injury and mental health needs. Our service mainly focuses on providing tenancy based community support and intensive support for adults who have required low-medium secure care for long periods and who display behaviours that challenge. Intensive support is provided through high standard domiciliary care and placements that have been carefully chosen close to public transport, shops and other services to ensure greater integration into the community.

We also provide training resources for other businesses in the same or similar field. Whether you are part of an organisation that has been running for years or you are just starting out, we provide resources and knowledge for contract procurement, staff training and development and business development.
